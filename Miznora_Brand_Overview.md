## 2. Product concept

**Miznora** is a proposed cloud-based platform to help salon and spa businesses manage day-to-day operations from one place. The product should prioritize simplicity, an elegant experience, and workflows relevant to Oman and GCC operators.

**Proposed one-line description:**  
*An all-in-one salon and spa management platform for appointments, billing, staff, inventory, customers and business insights.*

### Target audiences

- Salon and spa owners and managers
- Front-desk and reception staff
- Barbershops and beauty service providers
- Multi-location salon and spa groups
- Staff and service professionals

## 3. Proposed feature scope

These are **planned product directions**, not claims about currently implemented features.

### Core / MVP candidates

1. **Appointment booking and calendar** — manage staff schedules, services and appointments.
2. **Customer management (CRM)** — profiles, visit history and preferences.
3. **POS and billing** — service checkout, receipts, discounts and OMR pricing.
4. **Staff management** — roles, working hours, service assignments and commissions.
5. **Services and pricing** — service catalogue, packages and durations.
6. **Basic reporting** — daily sales, appointments and staff performance.
7. **Multi-tenant SaaS foundation** — separate business accounts and role-based access.

### Later-phase opportunities

- Inventory and stock management
- Memberships, gift cards and loyalty rewards
- WhatsApp confirmations and reminders, subject to provider rules and messaging costs
- Multi-branch management and consolidated reporting
- Online booking pages and customer self-service
- Advanced analytics and marketing campaigns
- Recurring subscriptions and integrated payments

## 4. Oman / GCC considerations

- **Languages:** English and Arabic, with right-to-left (RTL) layout support.
- **Currency:** Omani rial (OMR), with support for three decimal places where appropriate.
- **Localization:** local phone formats, time zones, dates and invoice presentation.
- **Payments and invoicing:** validate local payment-provider options and applicable tax/invoicing requirements before implementation.
- **Messaging:** WhatsApp can be an important customer communication channel; design for opt-in, templates and usage fees.
- **Growth:** retain country-aware settings so the product can expand across GCC markets.

## 5. Brand identity

### Visual direction

Premium, elegant, contemporary and Arabic-friendly. The initial logo concept uses an **M monogram** with petal/lotus-inspired lines; the app icon pairs the emblem with a **deep emerald** background and **champagne gold** accents.

**Suggested palette** (working direction; exact color values not finalized):

- Deep emerald green — primary brand color
- Champagne gold — accent and emblem
- Warm ivory — neutral background
- Dark charcoal — text and UI contrast

**Voice:** polished, clear, approachable and professional. Avoid overly ornate styling in the software UI; accessibility and usability take priority.

### Generated brand assets

- **Logo:** `champagne_gold_miznora_spa_logo.png`
- **App icon:** `emerald_gold_lotus_m_icon.png`

These are initial raster concepts. Before production, create vetted vector masters (SVG), light/dark variants, monochrome versions, and properly sized app icons/favicons. Verify legibility at small sizes.

## 7. Proposed platform structure

- **Business application:** salon/spa owners, managers, receptionists and staff.
- **SaaS administration:** tenant onboarding, subscription plans, billing, account status and support.
- **Customer booking experience (optional):** customer-facing booking page, with marketplace/discovery functionality considered separately.
- **Integrations:** payment gateway, messaging, email and reporting where required.

**Architecture and technology stack:** to be decided during requirements and technical design; no technology has been finalized in this brief.
